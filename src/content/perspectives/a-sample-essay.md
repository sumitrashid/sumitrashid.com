This is a sample essay. It exists so the site has something to show, and so you can see how a piece of writing is put together.

Everything between the two lines of dashes at the top is the *details* of the article: its title, date, a one-sentence summary, a category and some tags. Everything below them is the writing itself.

## Writing in Markdown

Markdown is plain text with a few simple symbols. Put two hash marks before a line to make a heading. Put asterisks around a word to make it *italic*, or two asterisks to make it **bold**.

> A line that begins with this symbol becomes a quotation.

To publish a new piece, add a new file like this one to the folder, change the details at the top, and write your text underneath.
